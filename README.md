## 🚀 About Me

I'm **Domz Lillo**, the **Founder & CEO** of [**Autobit**](https://github.com/autobit) — a fast-moving startup at the frontier of **Robotics** and **Machine Learning**. We build intelligent systems that don't just automate — they *think*, *detect*, and *protect*.

With a passionate team of engineers and innovators, Autobit is developing groundbreaking platforms focused on **Safety**, **Truth**, and **Intelligence** — redefining what's possible in autonomous systems.

---

## 🏢 Autobit — Who We Are

> *"Fast startup. Deep tech. Real impact."*

Autobit is a robotics and machine learning development team pushing the boundaries of what intelligent systems can do. We are builders, problem solvers, and pioneers operating at the intersection of hardware intelligence and AI-driven software.

**Our Mission:** Build trustworthy, high-performance autonomous systems that make the world safer and smarter.

## 🛡️ Our Platform: Safety & Truth

Autobit's platform is built on two foundational pillars:

- **Safety** — Proactive, AI-driven hazard detection and risk mitigation for dangerous environments
- **Truth** — Transparent, verifiable data systems that ensure accuracy and accountability in autonomous decision-making
